Axiom app icon (axiom.svg and every file derived from it in this directory:
axiom-1024.png, axiom.icns, axiom.ico and hicolor/).

This is Axiom's own icon, an "A" with an inspection probe on its crossbar, drawn for this project.
It replaces the upstream raven icon, which was not open source. It is covered by the repository's
MIT OR Apache-2.0 license. File names keep the upstream "axiom" stem until the packaging
and bundle identifiers are migrated.
